# Expert-annotation governance record

The software-history, contract-reconstruction, controlled-conformance, and runtime-triangulation components use public software artefacts.

The completed semantic-reproducibility exercise involved two non-author technical experts who classified public software-contract evidence. The public research artefact retains only anonymised annotator codes, broad professional-background descriptors, and annotation outputs; it does not publish direct identifiers or sensitive personal data.

Both annotators consented to participate in the coding exercise and to publication of their anonymised annotation outputs and broad professional-background descriptors.

The authors have indicated that, under their institutional assessment, this non-interventional expert-evaluation activity does not require a formal human-subjects ethics approval number. The repository therefore does **not** invent or claim an ethics approval/exemption identifier that the authors do not possess.

For manuscript submission the authors use the factual statement that the human contribution was a non-interventional expert-coding exercise over public software-contract artefacts, that no sensitive personal data, private records, clinical data, or vulnerable-population data were collected, and that informed consent for participation and anonymised publication was obtained from both annotators.

The authors remain responsible for ensuring that this wording is consistent with applicable University of the West of Scotland policy and journal-specific requirements.
