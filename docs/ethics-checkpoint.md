# Expert-annotation ethics checkpoint

The original software-history study used public repository evidence and did not include
human participants. The proposed independent expert-annotation study changes that
governance situation because it introduces human annotators.

Before recruitment:
- obtain the institutionally applicable written ethics determination, exemption, or
  approval;
- do not retain a blanket “no humans involved” statement without checking that
  determination;
- collect only professional-background metadata necessary to interpret annotator
  expertise and independence;
- avoid unnecessary personal or sensitive data in the public artefact;
- publish anonymised labels/metadata only to the extent permitted by the determination
  and participant information/consent process.

This repository does not claim an ethics approval or exemption until the authors have
obtained and recorded the applicable institutional decision.
